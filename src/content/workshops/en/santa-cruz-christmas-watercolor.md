Step into the **festive season** with a **relaxed watercolour workshop** in a charming ceramic shop, where creativity and craftsmanship meet. In this 2.5-hour experience, you'll **paint your own Christmas cards and gift tags** — the kind of small handmade touches that make the people you love feel truly seen.

Practice, play, and create a **little collection of your own** with gentle guidance and personal support throughout the session.

Come on your own, with a friend, or make it your **December outing together**.

**No previous experience is needed** — this workshop is perfect for beginners, dreamers, and anyone who has always wanted to try watercolour but never dared. **All materials are included**, and you'll leave with a set of cards and tags ready to give, plus a renewed sense of creative confidence.
